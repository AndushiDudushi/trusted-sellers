Buyer guide

How to buy

Everything on this site is a real product from a trusted factory seller. You don't check out here — you order directly from the seller, who sends you real QC photos before you pay. Here's exactly how it works.

Direct ordering guide

  1. 1

    Open a ticket

    Join the Discord and open a ticket in 🎫 support-ticket. This is your private line to the seller's helpers.

  2. 2

    Send style & size

    In the ticket, send the shoe you want (a link or screenshot from this site is perfect) and your size.

  3. 3

    A helper picks it up

    Helpers are on during working hours, 20:00 – 7:00 (GMT+8), and reply in your exclusive ticket.

  4. 4

    Get price + real QC

    They calculate the price and send you real QC photos of the actual pair they have — so you see the exact shoe before paying.

  5. 5

    Pay for the shoes

    You pay the shoe price first. Shipping is separate — the factory isn't the courier, so the exact shipping cost is only known once the pair reaches the airport.

  6. 6

    Fill in your details

    Provide your basic shipping information so the order can be prepared and dispatched.

  7. 7

    Shoes reach the airport

    The pair is moved to the airport. You get QC photos of it there, and the airport calculates your exact shipping cost.

  8. 8

    Pay shipping & track

    You pay the shipping fee, the pair ships to your country, and you receive a tracking number to follow it.

  9. 9

    Receive & review

    Your shoes arrive. Drop a photo in 🌟 reviews to help the next buyer.

New to this? Quick words

A few terms you'll see around the site and in your ticket.

QC photos

"Quality Check" photos — real pictures of the exact pair you're buying, taken before it ships. You approve them before paying shipping.

Yupoo

The seller's photo album site. Every product here links to its Yupoo album so you can see more colours and angles.

Weidian

A Chinese marketplace some listings link to directly. You can order through Discord instead if you prefer a guided checkout.

Batch

A version of the shoe from a specific factory line. Pricier batches are higher quality — pick the batch that fits your budget on each product page.
