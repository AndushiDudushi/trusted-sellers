Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Trusted Sellers (“we”, “the site”) is a showcase catalog that links you to independent third-party sellers. It's built to collect as little data as possible. Here's exactly what happens.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, logins or profiles.
  • No checkout or payments on this site — you order directly from each seller (WhatsApp, Discord, Weidian, Yupoo).
  • We don't ask for or store your name, email, address or payment details.

Stored in your browser

To make the site usable, a few things are saved in your browser's local storage — on your device only, never sent to us:

  • Your cart and saved (hearted) items.
  • Your currency preference.

You can clear these any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first, cookieless tool. It measures aggregate traffic — page views, referrers, rough location — without cookies and without collecting personal data or tracking you across other sites.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The site only uses functional local storage (above), which isn't used to profile you — so no cookie banner is needed.

Third-party links

Order and photo links take you to third parties — Weidian, Yupoo, WhatsApp, Discord, DHL, 17track and others. Once you leave, their own privacy policies apply. We don't control them.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Because we don't collect personal data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete for you. To remove locally-stored data, clear your browser's site data. Questions? Reach us on Discord.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date above will change.
