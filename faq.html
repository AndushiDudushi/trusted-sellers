Frequently asked questions

New to this? Start here. For the full ordering walkthrough, see the How to buy page.

What is Trusted Sellers?

A curated catalog that shows real, in-hand photos and prices from a small set of trusted third-party sellers (sneakers, designer bags, tech and apparel). You browse here, then order directly from the seller.

Are these authentic?

These are factory-quality pieces from independent sellers, not items sold or authenticated by the original brands. Every listing is QC-checked and photographed in-hand so you see exactly what you're getting before you pay.

How do I order?

Add items to your cart, then use the seller's order button (WhatsApp, Discord or their Weidian shop) — it opens a chat pre-filled with your items. The seller confirms stock, size, price and shipping. Full steps are on the How to buy page.

What are Weidian, Yupoo, batches and QC?

Yupoo is the seller's photo album. Weidian is a Chinese marketplace some listings link to. A batch is a factory version — pricier batches are higher quality. QC = quality-check photos of your exact item before it ships.

How much is shipping, and how long?

Shipping is quoted separately by the seller once your item reaches the airport — it isn't included in the item price shown here. Delivery is typically tracked via DHL / 17track. Times vary by destination.

What payment methods are accepted?

This depends on the seller — commonly PayPal or Remitly, or direct Weidian checkout. You pay the seller directly; no payment is taken on this site.

Why do prices show a currency switcher, and why “approximate”?

Sellers price in Chinese yuan (¥). We convert to $/€ as a guide so you can compare — the seller confirms the exact final price at order time.

What if there's a problem with my order?

Because orders are handled directly by each seller, returns and issues go through them (and their QC photos before shipping help avoid surprises). Message the seller you ordered from.

Is my data safe?

There are no accounts and no on-site payments. Your cart, saved items and currency live only in your browser. See the Privacy Policy.
